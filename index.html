Executive Summary

ESO enters Q1 2026 with its strongest operational foundation to date. After peaking at 19 staff and $3.86M revenue in 2023, leadership right-sized to 10 FTEs. Revenue per employee grew from $28K to $150K and net margin reached 22% in 2025. With $8.19M deployed across 191 businesses and delinquency below 5%, the fund is healthy. Five core systems are now live including Agentic AI. At 50.4% of the annual revenue goal confirmed before Q2 pipeline closes, ESO is no longer building. We are scaling.
